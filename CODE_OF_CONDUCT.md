# Code of conduct

Paneless follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- Be respectful and kind.
- Assume good intent.
- Keep discussions about the work.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- No publishing others' private information.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that don't follow this, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously break it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private reporting). Reports are handled confidentially.
